# FriendCircle ✳

**No scrolling. More showing up.**

FriendCircle helps people find their local circle and make plans to meet face to face. The app is for the invite, the meetup, and the memories after it—not an endless feed.

[Explore the demo](https://rkhan77.github.io/FriendCircle/) · [Member workspace](https://rkhan77.github.io/FriendCircle/app/)

## The vibe

- **Find your people.** See which friends are in your suburb and discover public profiles nearby through shared interests. Friends' exact locations stay private.
- **Make a plan.** Use a chat request or meal invitation to arrange a real catch up. New connections get a limited chat allowance so the conversation can move offline.
- **Show up.** Time spent together earns chat characters and social credits. Your circle can also unlock achievements by connecting across suburbs.
- **Eat together.** Catch Up shows restaurant offers within 5 km on the map. Bring more friends to unlock the next tier: 10% for two diners, 15% for three, and 20% for four or more when an approved offer is available.
- **Your profile, your call.** Add a photo, bio and interests, and choose public or private visibility.
